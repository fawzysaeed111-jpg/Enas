INTERMITTENT FASTING (16:8) SCHEDULE:
• Fasting Window: 8:00 PM → 12:00 PM (next day)
• Eating Window: 12:00 PM → 8:00 PM (only 3 main meals + 1 snack)
• Training Days (SATURDAY, SUNDAY, WEDNESDAY - High Carb): Add carbs (rice, bread, potato, fruits) to lunch/pre-workout
• Rest Days (MONDAY, TUESDAY, THURSDAY, FRIDAY - Low Carb): Focus on protein, fats, and vegetables. Remove rice/pasta/bread.
• Mama's Food Rule: Take half your normal plate of carbs, double the protein and salad.
• Hunger Control: Drink water/coffee/tea during fasting window. Your willpower is strong 💪
